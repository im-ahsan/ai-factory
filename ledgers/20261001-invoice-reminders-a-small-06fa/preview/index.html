Serves 5 requirements
  • REQ-1 When an authenticated staff user requests the overdue list, the invoice reminders API shall return every invoice that has the open status and a due date and for which today's date in the configured business timezone is on or after its due date plus the configured overdue threshold (default 7 days).
  • REQ-2 If an invoice has any status other than open in the existing invoice data (including paid, closed, void, cancelled, draft or written off) or has no due date, then the invoice reminders API shall leave it out of the overdue list.
  • REQ-4 When the overdue list is requested, the invoice reminders API shall return, for each overdue invoice, its invoice number, customer name, amount, due date and days overdue, where days overdue is the number of whole calendar days from the due date to today in the business timezone.
  • REQ-5 When the overdue list is requested with a customer filter, the invoice reminders API shall return only overdue invoices belonging to that customer.
  • REQ-6 When the overdue list is requested with a days-overdue bucket filter of 7–30, 31–60, 61–90 or 91 and over (90+), the invoice reminders API shall return only overdue invoices whose days overdue falls inside that bucket, bounds included.
