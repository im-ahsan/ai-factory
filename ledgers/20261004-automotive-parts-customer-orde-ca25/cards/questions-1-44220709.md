Run 20261004-automotive-parts-customer-orde-ca25. Your request:
> # Automotive Parts Customer Ordering Portal
> 
> ## 1. Product Overview
> 
> A web-based B2B ordering portal for a mid-size automotive parts distributor.
> 
> The portal allows automotive workshops, dealerships, fleet operators, and other approved business customers to browse the distributor's parts catalogue, check customer-specific pricing and stock availability, place and track orders, and download invoices.
> 
> Distributor office staff manage customers, orders, inventory visibility, pricing, invoices, and operational reporting.
> 
> Administrators manage users, catalogue data, compatibility information, customer accounts, pricing structures, and system configuration.
> 
> There is no existing application code. The technology stack has not yet been decided.
> 
> ---
> 
> ## 2. Product Goals
> 
> The primary goals are:
> 
> - Replace phone/email-based parts ordering with a self-service ordering portal.
> - Allow customers to quickly find parts using part numbers, product names, categories, and vehicle information.
> - Show each customer their applicable pricing.
> - Show current stock availability based on the distributor's ERP stock feed.
> - Reduce manual order-entry work for office staff.
> - Give customers visibility into order status and invoices.
> - Provide staff with operational order queues and delivery information.
> - Provide management with basic sales and product reporting.
> - Maintain an auditable history of orders, invoices, customer accounts, and catalogue changes.
>

**Q-1** Is the Customer Account Manager role part of the first release?
  A. No: first release has Buyer, Office Staff and Administrator only   ← recommended: The request calls it optional and an open point. Leaving it out is the smallest complete scope.
  B. Yes: build Account Manager alongside Buyer
  C. Build the role model so Account Manager can be added later, but don't expose it now
  (why it matters: Decides who can see which company's orders and invoices.)

**Q-2** Should a Buyer see every order and invoice for their company, or only the orders they placed (plus their invoices)?
  A. Buyer sees all orders and invoices of their company   ← recommended: Section 3.1 says the buyer can 'View their company's orders', and staff-created orders must show in the customer's history.
  B. Buyer sees only orders they placed and the invoices for those orders
  (why it matters: Controls who sees which orders and invoices.)

**Q-3** How does an invoice become Overdue?
  A. Unpaid past a single configurable payment term (e.g. 30 days from invoice date)   ← recommended: Overdue is required, and one global setting needs no extra customer fields.
  B. Payment terms set per customer company
  C. No Overdue status in the first release
  (why it matters: Payment status shown to customers and staff.)

**Q-4** Which customer-account and catalogue changes go in the audit history, and who can view it?
  A. Create, edit and retire on products, categories, price lists/prices, customer companies and users, with who/when and changed fields; admins only   ← recommended: Matches I-8, and section 3.4 gives audit viewing to admins.
  B. Who/when only, no field-level detail; admins only
  C. Same as the first option, but staff can also view it
  (why it matters: Write records, and who can see them.)

**Q-5** Can customers create or edit their own delivery addresses?
  A. No: customers select from addresses managed by staff/admin   ← recommended: The buyer capability is only 'View their company's addresses'.
  B. Yes: buyers can add and edit their company's addresses
  C. Customers can request changes, and staff approve them
  (why it matters: Customer write permission on address records.)
