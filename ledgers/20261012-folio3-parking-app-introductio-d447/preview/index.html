Parking App

Employees sign in with Google (S-1). If the corporate profile can't be fetched, they land on a recoverable error page (S-2). A first-time non-dedicated employee registers a plate (S-3) before reaching parking. Dedicated holders use Today (S-4), Releases (S-6) and My profile (S-9) to release or schedule their reserved space. Non-dedicated employees use Available spaces (S-5), get push alerts, claim a space with one of their own vehicles (S-7) and receive a floor pass that can't be cancelled (S-8). They manage plates in My vehicles (S-10). Security guards watch the fail-closed entrance console (S-11) and the entry log (S-12). Parking Administrators manage employees (S-13, S-14), spaces (S-15), releases and reversals (S-16), operating calendar, release time and report settings (S-17), and filtered, exportable utilization reports (S-18).
